# Umami — Social Media Content Plan / Sosyal Medya İçerik Planı

---

## EN — Strategy Overview

**Platform Focus:** Instagram (primary), LinkedIn (secondary)

**Posting Frequency:** 4 posts per week on Instagram, 1 post per week on LinkedIn

**Content Pillars:**
- Food — 40% (dishes, ingredients, preparation)
- Atmosphere — 30% (interior, mood, lighting)
- Philosophy — 20% (brand voice, Japanese culture, food stories)
- Promotion — 10% (reservations, special menus, events)

**Tone of Voice Reminder:**
- Short sentences
- No exclamation marks
- No emojis in the caption body, only at the very end if necessary
- Never say "delicious" or "amazing" — show, don't tell

---

## TR — Strateji Özeti

**Platform Odağı:** Instagram (birincil), LinkedIn (ikincil)

**Paylaşım Sıklığı:** Instagram'da haftada 4 gönderi, LinkedIn'de haftada 1 gönderi

**İçerik Sütunları:**
- Yemek — yüzde 40 (yemekler, malzemeler, hazırlık)
- Atmosfer — yüzde 30 (iç mekan, ruh hali, aydınlatma)
- Felsefe — yüzde 20 (marka sesi, Japon kültürü, yemek hikayeleri)
- Promosyon — yüzde 10 (rezervasyon, özel menüler, etkinlikler)

## EN — LinkedIn Weekly Post

Template: LinkedIn Banner (1584x396px)
Posting day: Thursday
Pillar: Philosophy + Brand Story

**Week 1:**
```
We built a restaurant brand using only AI tools.

No Photoshop. No stock photos. No templates.

Every visual was generated. Every word was intentional.
The result is Umami — a Japanese fusion restaurant identity
that feels handcrafted, not machine-made.

This is what AI-assisted branding looks like in 2024.

#branding #aidesign #visualidentity #japanesefusion #freelancedesign
```

**Week 2:**
```
The hardest part of AI-generated branding is not the tools.
It is knowing when to stop.

Every prompt we wrote for Umami had one rule:
less is more.

That is also the Japanese way.

#umami #brandidentity #minimalistdesign #aitools #designprocess
```

**Week 3:**
```
Color is a decision, not a decoration.

For Umami, we chose five colors.
Each one has a name. Each one has a reason.
None of them are bright.

This is what intentional brand design looks like.

#colorpalette #branddesign #visualidentity #designthinking #umami
```

**Week 4:**
```
Typography is the voice of a brand before anyone reads a word.

For Umami, we chose two typefaces.
One serif. One sans-serif.
They never compete. They always agree.

#typography #brandidentity #fontdesign #umami #freelancebranding
```

## TR — Genel Notlar

Caption yazarken dikkat etmen gereken kurallar:

Hiçbir zaman "muhteşem", "harika", "enfes" gibi kelimeler kullanma. Bunlar marka sesine aykırı.

Her caption kısa cümlelerden oluşsun. Uzun paragraf yok.

Hashtag sayısını Instagram'da 5 ile sınırla. Daha fazlası spam gibi görünür.

LinkedIn'de hashtag kullan ama caption sonuna koy, caption içine yerleştirme.

Story'lerde her zaman bir harekete geçirici öğe olsun: "Link in bio", "Swipe up", "Reserve now" gibi.
